Checkpoint 1.8.

Let \(T: V \to V\) be an operator on an inner product space. Let \(\vec{v}\) be an eigenvector of \(T\) with eigenvalue \(\lambda\text{.}\) Show that
\begin{equation*} \langle \vec{v}, T(\vec{v})\rangle = \langle T(\vec{v}), \vec{v}\rangle = \lambda \|\vec{v}\|^{2}. \end{equation*}
