What should the averages approach?

For a fair coin, these exact expectations give the simulation a target. Random runs will wobble around them and settle closer as the trial count grows.

H

First heads

Each flip has a 1/2 chance of ending the wait, so this is geometric with success probability 1/2.

E[N] = 1/p = 2
T

First tails

By symmetry, waiting for the first tails has exactly the same distribution as waiting for the first heads.

E[N] = 2
HH

First consecutive heads

A lone H puts you partway there, but a T resets the streak. The overlap structure makes the mean longer than 4 flips.

E[N] = 6
H↔T

First switch

The first flip establishes a side. After that, each new flip has a 1/2 chance to differ from the previous one.

E[N] = 1 + 2 = 3
