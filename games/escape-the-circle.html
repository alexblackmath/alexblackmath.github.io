Rules & mathematics

Score

Count tiles, not distance. The start counts as 1; each move adds 1, including revisits. Leaving adds 0. Vertex-crossing and edge-following rays are forbidden.

Ray vs. route

All cells are convex. Every legal ray follows edge-adjacent cells without revisiting one, so the shortest free route is no longer than the shortest ray.

Optimality

In 2D, ray counts are checked between consecutive vertex directions; free routes use breadth-first search. Higher-dimensional targets use matching global lower bounds and playable solutions, not sampled minima.

In 3D and 4D, minimize across all slices. Each attempt stays in one plane; the two parts may use different planes. The 4D sliders cover a two-parameter family, not every plane. Both global optima are attainable with these controls. Ambiguous sections are rejected.

How to play

Escape from the origin through the fewest tiles. The start counts as 1; leaving adds 0. Revisits count again.

A · Ray

Aim with the pointer, touch, or angle slider. Press Launch ray. Rays through vertices—including rim vertices—or along edges are forbidden. Avoid vertices adjusts blocked aims; switching it off does not allow them.

B · Free

Click highlighted neighbors or use their buttons. Neighbors must share an edge, not just a corner. Reach a rim tile, then press Escape. Use Undo to revise your route.

Progress

Each stage begins with a fixed Medium board. Find both optimal escapes, in either order, to unlock the next stage and random boards. Each optimum stays hidden until you submit it.

Levels

1: 2D. 2: 3D with one rotation slider. 3: 4D with two sliders.

In 3D and 4D, find the optimum across all slices. Each attempt stays in one plane; the two parts may use different planes. Reset an unfinished attempt before rotating. Best scores remain saved.

The 4D controls cover a limited family of planes, with both global optima attainable.

Practice

After mastery, use New subdivision, Size, and Seed. Link shares the board, not scores or unlocks. Progress is saved in this browser.

Keyboard controls

Focus the board. Ray: ← / → aim by 1° (Shift: 0.1°); ↑ / ↓ select safe directions. Free: ← / → select a neighbor. Enter launches, moves, or escapes. U undoes; R resets; H hints.

Reset progress?
